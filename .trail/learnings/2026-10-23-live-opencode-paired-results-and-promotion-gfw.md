## Watch out

Four calls validate integration behavior, not statistical model quality or deployment of all upstream datasets. The evaluator remains an external claim when imported into Agent Profiles by design, even though these measurements come from real native runs. Default CI must remain credential-free. Broad historical Trail vocabulary matches do not negate measured assertions; preserve the production ownership and immutable input safeguards.
